Wall Magazine — SPECTRUM

SPECTRUM is entirely student-made — posters, illustrations, physics explainers, quizzes and puzzles that students research, design and put up themselves. It's as much a showcase of creativity as it is of physics, and it's refreshed with new editions through the year.

Beyond the classroom

Student Clubs

A degree is more than lectures and examinations. Some of the best parts of student life are what you build alongside your studies — a skill, a friendship, a project, a voice. Our department clubs are groups of students who come together around something they enjoy, learn from one another, and grow the talents that no syllabus can teach: creativity, teamwork, leadership and confidence.

Whether you love explaining science, caring for a garden, writing poems, framing a photograph, arguing a point or gazing at the stars — there is a place for you. The clubs are just being formed, so your interest and ideas will shape them. Have an interest that is not listed? Write to us at vdu.physics@gmail.com and we will see how to start it.
