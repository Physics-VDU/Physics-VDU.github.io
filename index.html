About the department

Grounded in Koraput, built for research

Established in 1963, the Department of Physics has grown from its beginnings as part of Vikram Deb College — founded in 1947 — into a premier department of Vikram Dev University, the primary centre of higher education for the undivided Koraput district.

Situated in Koraput, a tribal-dominated district, the department is committed to widening access to physics education and research for underrepresented groups, and to contributing to the region's development.

Vision

Empowering students from diverse backgrounds, especially from tribal and rural areas, to become leaders in physics education, research and innovation, fostering inclusive growth and societal transformation.

Mission

To provide quality physics education that cultivates critical thinking, creativity and problem-solving — by fostering an inclusive learning environment, promoting research and innovation, building collaborations with institutions and industry, addressing societal needs through physics applications, and strengthening faculty expertise and student employability.

Curriculum

Aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, the curriculum emphasises interdisciplinary learning, research and skill development; implementation began in the 2024–25 academic session.

Academic activities

The department regularly arranges seminars, invited lectures by visiting experts, and student presentations and discussions — enhancing learning, fostering research, and encouraging intellectual exchange.

Research areas

Materials Science & Thin Films

Nanomaterials synthesis, thin-film deposition, sputtered metal nitrides, perovskite solar cells and photodetectors, and thin-film-based sensing.

Dr. Aishwarya Madhuri · Dr. Santilata Sahoo · Dr. Lipsa Rani Karna

Computational & Theoretical Physics

Fluid dynamics, biophysics and quantum computing; density functional theory, electronic spin transport and 2D materials.

Dr. Paramananda Padhi · Dr. Alok Ranjan Sahoo

Photonics & Sensors

Biosensors, fiber-optic sensors and nanophotonics.

Dr. Preeti Das

Single-Molecule Biophysics

Intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs), single-molecule FRET, fluorescence correlation spectroscopy (FCS), and liquid–liquid phase separation (LLPS).

Dr. Bikash Chandra Swain

Semiconductor Physics & Optoelectronics

III-nitride semiconductors, pyroelectric effects and thermal transport, and optoelectronic applications.

Dr. Gopal Hansdah

Beyond the classroom

Student activities

A space for students to put their own curiosity and creativity on display, alongside the department's talks and lectures.

Wall Magazine — SPECTRUM

SPECTRUM is entirely student-made — posters, illustrations, physics explainers, quizzes and puzzles that students research, design and put up themselves. It's as much a showcase of creativity as it is of physics, and it's refreshed with new editions through the year.
